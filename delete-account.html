Delete your Home Planner account

Home Planner by RCDevelopmentZ

Delete in the app

  1. Open Home Planner and sign in.
  2. Go to the Household tab and open Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately.

Delete by email

If you can't access the app, email rcdevelopmentz.apps@gmail.com from the email address you used to sign up, with the subject "Delete my Home Planner account". We will delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • your account (email address, password and sign-in details);
  • your membership in every home and your device notification tokens;
  • any home you created that has no other members, along with all of its content (rooms, chores, appointments, grocery lists, money entries and so on).

What is kept

  • If a home you created has other members, ownership passes to another member, and the shared household content stays available to them.
  • Deleted data may remain in backups for a limited time before it is overwritten.

See our Privacy Policy for more information.
