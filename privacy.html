Privacy Policy

Home Planner · Effective date: 5 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how RCDevelopmentZ ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects information when you use the Home Planner mobile app (the "App"). If you have any questions, contact us at rcdevelopmentz.apps@gmail.com.

Information we collect

Account information. When you create an account we collect your email address and a password. Passwords are stored only in hashed form by our authentication provider; we cannot see them. If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from Google.

Household content you enter. The App stores the information you and other members of your home add, such as:

  • home name and household members (name, and optionally birthday, phone number, color and notes);
  • rooms, walls, furniture and room plans;
  • chores and chore history;
  • appointments and reminders;
  • grocery lists and items;
  • money entries such as transactions, budgets, savings goals and deposits.

Device information for notifications. If you allow notifications, we store a push notification token for your device so we can send reminders and household updates.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or files. The App does not contain advertising and does not use analytics or tracking tools.

How we use information

  • to create and secure your account and let you sign in;
  • to store your household data and share it with the members of your home;
  • to send notifications you have enabled, such as reminders;
  • to respond to your support requests.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

Sharing

Content you add to a home is visible to the other members of that home. We share data only with the service providers that run the App on our behalf:

  • Supabase: account authentication and database hosting;
  • Expo and Google Firebase Cloud Messaging: delivery of push notifications;
  • Google: only if you choose to sign in with Google.

We may also disclose information if required by law.

Storage and security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored with our hosting provider. Access to household data is restricted so that only members of a home can read it. Some data may also be cached on your device so the App works quickly. No method of storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the App, or by request; see Delete your account. When you delete your account, your account and personal data are permanently removed. If you created a home that has other members, ownership of that home is passed to another member and the shared household content remains available to them. Deleted data may remain in backups for a limited time before it is overwritten.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, or to object to its processing. You can edit most data directly in the App. For anything else, email us and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and change the effective date above.
